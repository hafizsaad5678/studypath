info Overview

The Master of Science in Computer Science at Politecnico di Milano aims to train highly qualified professionals who can manage the complexity of modern IT systems. The program provides a solid methodological background along with deep technical knowledge in areas like Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Cybersecurity.

account_balance Tuition & Funding

Base tuition is approximately €3,800 per year for non-EU students. However, Polimi offers several merit-based scholarships (Platinum, Gold, Silver) that can significantly reduce or waive these fees, alongside regional DSU grants based on financial need.
